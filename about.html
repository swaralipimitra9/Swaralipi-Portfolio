MY BACKGROUND

SIX YEARS OF ACADEMIC WRITING EXPERIENCE

Learning has never belonged to just one subject.

My academic background includes an undergraduate degree in Anthropology and postgraduate study in Dietetics and Food Service Management through IGNOU. These areas have shaped my interest in people, health, behaviour and the connections between individuals and society.

Alongside my academic interests, I enjoy exploring topics in biology, management, marketing, political science, geography and technical writing. This multidisciplinary outlook allows me to approach subjects with curiosity, look for connections and adapt my writing to different audiences.

I am especially interested in research-based writing: understanding a topic, reviewing relevant information, organising ideas and presenting them in a clear, structured way.
