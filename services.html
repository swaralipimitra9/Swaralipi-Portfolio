HOW I CAN HELP

Thoughtful work.

Research, writing, learning and creative support across a range of subjects. Every project begins with understanding your goals and the audience you want to reach.

01 / RESEARCH

Research & Academic Support

Structured support for research-focused projects, from exploring a topic to organising findings and presenting information clearly.

  • Literature review support and source organisation
  • Research methodology guidance
  • Questionnaire and research-tool support
  • Excel and SPSS data organisation and interpretation
  • Thesis and project editing for clarity and structure

Support is provided transparently and ethically. I do not offer ghostwriting, fabricated data, or work intended to be submitted as someone else's independent academic work.

02 / WRITING

Content & Blog Writing

Readable, well-organised content shaped around the topic, purpose and intended audience.

  • Blog articles and educational explainers
  • Website and service-page content
  • Research-based subject articles
  • Proofreading and language refinement
  • Technical and informational writing
03 / NUTRITION

Nutrition Education

Learning support that makes nutrition concepts easier to understand for students and curious readers.

  • Nutrition subject tutoring
  • Study notes and topic explanations
  • Educational articles and learning resources
  • Concept revision for relevant coursework

This is educational support, not individualised medical nutrition therapy or a substitute for care from an appropriately qualified clinician.

04 / CREATIVE

Creative Content Support

Practical content development for individuals, small businesses and digital platforms.

  • Content ideas and short-form video concepts
  • Basic reel editing using tools such as CapCut
  • Canva-based visual content
  • Captions and social media copy
